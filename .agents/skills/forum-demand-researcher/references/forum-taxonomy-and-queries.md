# Qualitative Forum Demand Research Taxonomy & Query Guide

## 1. Qualitative Research Framework (Braun & Clarke Thematic Analysis)

### Core Guardrail
Forum extracts serve strictly as **qualitative evidence of real-world user demand, anxiety, and confusion**. They are NEVER treated as legal authority or representative statistical market research.

## 2. 8-Point Qualitative Employee Anxiety Taxonomy

1. **Offer Fairness & Baseline Confusion**:
   - Emotional friction: Inability to know whether the offer is fair or insulting compared to statutory minimums.
   - User quote archetype: *"Employer offered 2 months pay. I have 6 years service. Is this normal or am I being ripped off?"*
2. **Fear of Offer Withdrawal (The "Blackmail" Dilemma)**:
   - Emotional friction: Terror that asking for more money will cause the employer to cancel the deal and dismiss them.
   - User quote archetype: *"If I counter-offer, can they just pull the settlement and fire me for gross misconduct?"*
3. **Artificial Deadline Pressure**:
   - Emotional friction: Employers setting arbitrary 48-hour or 72-hour deadlines.
   - User quote archetype: *"HR gave me 3 days to sign. I haven't even found a solicitor yet. What happens if I miss the deadline?"*
4. **Tax Deduction Shock (PENP Surprise)**:
   - Emotional friction: Anger and betrayal when the employer deducts tax from notice pay after HR promised "£30k is tax-free".
   - User quote archetype: *"HR said the first £30k is tax-free, but my final payslip has £4k in tax deducted from PILON!"*
5. **Solicitor Fee Contribution Shortfall**:
   - Emotional friction: Employer contributing £500 + VAT; solicitors quoting £800–£1,200.
   - User quote archetype: *"My employer's contribution is £500, but every solicitor I call wants £750+. Do I have to pay the rest myself?"*
6. **Restrictive Covenants & Gagging Anxieties**:
   - Emotional friction: Clauses prohibiting working for competitors or speaking out about mistreatment.
   - User quote archetype: *"They inserted a 12-month non-compete clause in the settlement. Can they stop me working in my industry?"*
7. **Agreed Reference Hesitations**:
   - Emotional friction: Fear of future career sabotage if the reference is only factual dates.
   - User quote archetype: *"Will future employers know I took a settlement? Why won't my boss give a positive reference?"*
8. **Solicitor Briefing Inefficiency**:
   - Operational friction: Showing up to the solicitor unprepared, wasting time explaining basic employment dates.
