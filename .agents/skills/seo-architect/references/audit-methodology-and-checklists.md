# SEO Audit Methodology & Verification Checklist

When conducting an SEO, AEO, or E-E-A-T audit on any SettlementCheck route, execute this deterministic 6-step workflow.

---

## Step 1: Statutory Accuracy Scan (Critical Priority)

Accuracy errors destroy trust and search rankings faster than any technical issue.
- Verify weekly pay cap is **£751 (GB)** and **£783 (NI)** (April 2026, SI 2026/310).
- Verify unfair dismissal compensatory award cap is **£123,543**.
- Verify tax-free termination threshold is **£30,000** under ITEPA 2003 s.403.
- Verify protective award max is **90 days** (TULRCA 1992 s.189), never 180 days.
- Verify PILON is never described as tax-free (it is fully taxable under s.402D).
- Flag any mention of "Employment Rights Act 2025" (provisions commence in stages from 2026).

---

## Step 2: E-E-A-T & Statutory Citation Check

Check for 6 core trust signals:
1. Primary statute citations with specific sections (e.g. ERA 1996 s.203(3), ITEPA 2003 s.403).
2. Outbound links to authoritative `legislation.gov.uk` sources.
3. Plain-English author review / regulatory attribution (SRA-regulated solicitor review standard).
4. Date of last statutory update prominently displayed.
5. Statutory educational disclaimer: independent calculation tool, not formal legal advice.
6. Zero overclaiming: no "guaranteed maximum payout" assertions.

---

## Step 3: Answer-First & GEO Defense

- **Sentence 1 Directness**: Opening paragraph must deliver the direct answer or statutory number within the first 35 words.
- **Visual Contrast Summary Box**: Summary box above the fold answering target AI Overview queries.
- **No Wall of Text**: Short paragraphs (2-3 sentences), scannable bullet points, and inline interactive widgets.

---

## Step 4: Title & Meta Calibration

- **Title Tag**: Strictly **50 to 60 characters** (including ` | SettlementCheck`). Must include year (`2026`) or numeric anchor (`£751`).
- **Meta Description**: Strictly **140 to 155 characters**. Must lead with Answer-First fact, include clear value proposition, and end with an empowering action.

---

## Step 5: Technical Invariants

- **Trailing Slash**: Every internal link and canonical URL must end with a trailing slash (`/`).
- **Canonical Domain**: Strictly non-www (`https://settlementcheck.co.uk/`).
- **Schema.org JSON-LD**: Validate `WebApplication`, `FAQPage`, or `Article` schema syntax.

---

## Step 6: Mobile CTA & Value-First Hierarchy

- Interactive calculator or primary action visible above the fold on mobile (375px/390px viewports).
- Zero gating: user must never be forced to submit an email before receiving baseline calculation results.
