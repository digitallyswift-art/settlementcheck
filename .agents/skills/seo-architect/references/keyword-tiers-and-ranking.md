Target queries are structured into four commercial and intent tiers:

- `settlement agreement calculator 2026`
- `is my settlement offer fair`
- `settlement calculator uk`

- `settlement agreement calculator free`

### Tier 2 — Secondary Specialist Terms (Specific Context)
- `redundancy pay cap 2026`
- `pilon tax uk 2026`
- `protective award redundancy calculator`
- `settlement agreement tax free 30000`
- `constructive dismissal settlement calculator`

### Tier 3 — GEO / AI Overview / Featured Snippet Targets (Zero-Click Answer Defense)
- `what is the redundancy pay cap 2026` → Direct statutory figure: **£751 per week** (SI 2026/310).
- `is pilon taxable uk` → Direct answer: **Yes, PILON is fully subject to Income Tax and Class 1 NICs** under ITEPA 2003 s.402D.
- `what is a protective award` → Up to **90 days' gross pay** under TULRCA 1992 s.189.
- `how long do you have to sign a settlement agreement` → ACAS Code benchmark: **Minimum 10 calendar days**.

### Tier 4 — Low Competition, High Conversion (Own Outright)
- `settlement offer below legal minimum`
- `settlement calculator no email required`
