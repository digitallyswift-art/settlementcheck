# SEO Competitor Matrix & Moat Strategy Reference

## 1. Competitive Landscape Overview

SettlementCheck targets high-commercial-intent organic queries related to settlement agreement calculations, unfair dismissal, and statutory redundancy in the UK.

Competitors fall into two distinct tiers:

### Tier 1: Direct Calculator Competitors (Displace First)

| Competitor | Domain | Core Weakness to Exploit |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Monaco Solicitors** | `monacosolicitors.co.uk` | Lead-gen gating before answers; copy heavily hedged for liability; refuses to provide an immediate named fairness verdict. |
| **Redmans Solicitors** | `redmans.co.uk` | Basic tool; lacks named fairness verdict; does not separate PILON and Statutory Redundancy Pay (SRP) tax treatments. |
| **KLG Law** | `klglaw.co.uk` | Splintered authority across multiple thin pages; explicitly disclaims that "calculator cannot assess fairness" (refusing what users search for). |
| **Winston Solicitors** | `winstonsolicitors.co.uk` | High domain authority but generic output; no net take-home estimation; no structured verdict badge. |
| **settlementagreementcalculator.co.uk** | Exact-match domain | Pure exact-match ranking advantage; archaic UI, no tax breakdown, no mobile optimization, zero emotional resonance. |
| **Tribunal Claim Solicitors** | `tribunalclaimsolicitors.co.uk` | Tax calculator only; does not evaluate settlement fairness or statutory compensation caps. |

### Tier 2: Authority Content Competitors (Displace on Directness)

| Competitor | Domain | Core Weakness to Exploit |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Citizens Advice** | `citizensadvice.org.uk` | High DA, but passive, non-interactive guidance; buries statutory figures in walls of text; written for bureaucratic completeness rather than an employee in shock. |
| **ACAS** | `acas.org.uk` | High authority on "settlement agreement calculator acas" queries, but ACAS provides no calculator tool at all. We capture this with ACAS-aligned calculation hubs. |

## 2. SettlementCheck's Core Moats

1. **Immediate Value Before Commitment**:
   - Zero gating. The employee gets the statutory redundancy baseline, notice pay, and commercial fairness estimate *before* any email or telephone prompt.
2. **Deterministic Named Verdict**:
   - Only tool providing clear commercial benchmarks: `BELOW_MINIMUM`, `BELOW_TYPICAL`, `WITHIN_RANGE`, `ABOVE_TYPICAL`.
3. **Statutory Tax Separation**:
   - Correctly splits statutory redundancy (under £30,000 exemption, ITEPA 2003 s.403) from PILON (fully taxable as earnings under s.402D).
4. **Empathetic UK Emotional Register**:
   - Written for someone receiving news at 11pm on a Tuesday. Crisp, under 20 words per sentence, zero corporate jargon, reassuring that employer covers legal fees under Section 203 ERA 1996.
