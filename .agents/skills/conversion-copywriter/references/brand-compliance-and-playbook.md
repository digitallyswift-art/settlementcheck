# Conversion Copywriter Brand Compliance & CTA Playbook

## 1. Brand Non-Negotiables (.claude/BRAND.md)

1. **Zero Em Dashes (—)**:
   - Never use em dashes (—). Use hyphens with spaces (` - `) or break into shorter sentences.
2. **Empathetic UK English**:
   - "Solicitor" (never lawyer, attorney, counsel).
   - "Redundancy" (never layoff).
   - "Employment tribunal" (never labour court, labor court, court).
   - "Settlement agreement" (never severance package).
3. **Key Fee Reassurance**:
   - Whenever mentioning independent legal advice, always reassure the employee:
     *"Your employer covers the legal fees (typically £500 to £1,500 + VAT)."*
4. **Banned Corporate Buzzwords**:
   - Never write: *"don't worry"*, *"reach out"*, *"touch base"*, *"leverage"*, *"essentially"*, *"basically"*, *"navigating the complexities"*, *"tapestry"*, *"delve"*.
5. **Zero Prompt / CRO Meta-Jargon**:
   - Never expose internal framework phrases in user-facing copy (e.g. *"Attention Economy"*, *"Diagnostic"*, *"Existential Vacuum"*, *"Cognitive Relief"*, *"Fast-Path"*).
   - Use plain, natural UK English that directly matches what the employee is experiencing.
6. **Sentence Length**:
   - Aim for under 20 words per sentence.
   - Max 2 to 3 sentences per paragraph.
7. **Strictly Descriptive (Zero Prescriptive Legal Advice)**:
   - SettlementCheck is an informational triage and solicitor-matching platform, not an authorised legal practice.
   - Never use prescriptive instructions ("what to say", "demand £X", "do not sign", "counter with").
   - Always frame figures as calculated baselines and formulate objective questions to explore with a qualified solicitor.

## 1.5. Prescriptive vs Descriptive Matrix

| Funnel Context | ❌ Prescriptive (Unauthorised / Risky) | ✅ Descriptive (Objective / Compliant) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Negotiation Section** | "What to say to your employer" | "Questions to consider with a solicitor" |
| **Target Figure** | "You should ask for £19,673" | "Calculated illustrative negotiation range: £19,673" |
| **Zero Offer State** | "The current offer is £0" / "Offer: £0" | "No employer offer recorded" (suppress counter-offer) |
| **Offer Evaluation** | "Do not accept this offer" | "Offer is below the calculated statutory baseline" |
| **Legal Contribution** | "Your employer is required to pay £500" | "Employers customarily contribute £350 to £750 + VAT" |
| **Email Template** | "Without Prejudice: Proposal" | "Discussion Agenda / Note of Figures" |
