# Conversion Copywriter Brand Compliance & CTA Playbook

## 1. Brand Non-Negotiables (.claude/BRAND.md)

1. **Zero Em Dashes (—)**:
   - Never use em dashes (—). Use hyphens with spaces (` - `) or break into shorter sentences.
2. **Empathetic UK English**:
   - "Solicitor" (never lawyer, attorney, counsel).
   - "Redundancy" (never layoff).
   - "Employment tribunal" (never labour court, labor court, court).
   - "Settlement agreement" (never severance package).
3. **Key Fee Reassurance**:
   - Whenever mentioning independent legal advice, always reassure the employee:
     *"Your employer covers the legal fees (typically £500 to £1,500 + VAT)."*
4. **Banned Corporate Buzzwords**:
   - Never write: *"don't worry"*, *"reach out"*, *"touch base"*, *"leverage"*, *"essentially"*, *"basically"*, *"navigating the complexities"*, *"tapestry"*, *"delve"*.
5. **Zero Prompt / CRO Meta-Jargon**:
   - Never expose internal framework phrases in user-facing copy (e.g. *"Attention Economy"*, *"Diagnostic"*, *"Existential Vacuum"*, *"Cognitive Relief"*, *"Fast-Path"*).
   - Use plain, natural UK English that directly matches what the employee is experiencing.
6. **Sentence Length**:
   - Aim for under 20 words per sentence.
   - Max 2 to 3 sentences per paragraph.
