# Conversion Copywriter Brand Compliance & CTA Playbook

## 1. Brand Non-Negotiables (.agents/rules/brand-standards.md)

1. **Zero Em Dashes (`—`)**:
   - Never use em dashes (`—`). Use hyphens with spaces (` - `) or break into shorter sentences.
2. **Empathetic UK English**:
   - `"Solicitor"` (never lawyer, attorney, counsel).
   - `"Redundancy"` (never layoff).
   - `"Employment tribunal"` (never labour court, labor court, court).
   - `"Settlement agreement"` (never severance package).
   - Direct `"you"` voice rather than stiff third-person phrasing (`"the employee"`).
3. **Legal Fee Transparency (CMA / SRA / DMCC Act 2024 Compliant)**:
   - When mentioning independent legal advice, never state that legal advice is unconditionally *"free"* or that employers are required to pay under Section 203.
   - Always state: *"In standard practice, employers offer a capped fee contribution (typically £350 to £500+ plus VAT) directly to your solicitor upon completion."* Note that advice exceeding the cap or abortive work remains subject to the solicitor's engagement terms.
4. **Banned Corporate Buzzwords & AI Slop**:
   - Never write: *"don't worry"*, *"reach out"*, *"touch base"*, *"leverage"*, *"essentially"*, *"basically"*, *"navigating the complexities"*, *"tapestry"*, *"delve"*, *"please be advised"*, *"going forward"*, *"it is important to note"*, *"when it comes to"*, *"ultimately"*, *"in today's fast-paced world"*.
5. **Zero Prompt / CRO Meta-Jargon**:
   - Never expose internal framework or B2B phrases in user-facing copy (e.g. *"Attention Economy"*, *"Diagnostic"*, *"Existential Vacuum"*, *"Cognitive Relief"*, *"Fast-Path"*, *"pre-flight intake dossiers"*).
   - Use plain, natural UK English that directly matches what the employee is experiencing.
6. **Sentence Length**:
   - Aim for under 20 words per sentence.
   - Max 2 to 3 sentences per paragraph.
7. **Strictly Descriptive (Zero Prescriptive Legal Advice)**:
   - SettlementCheck is an informational triage and solicitor-matching platform, not an authorised legal practice.
   - Never use prescriptive instructions (*"what to say"*, *"demand £X"*, *"do not sign"*, *"counter with"*).
   - Always frame figures as calculated baselines and formulate objective questions to explore with a qualified solicitor.

## 2. E-E-A-T Review Checklist for Guides

Before publishing or approving any guide page, verify:

- [ ] **Freshness & Scope Line**: Visible statutory update note near the H1 (e.g. `Updated for April 2026 UK statutory rates · Checked against ERA 1996 & ACAS Code 4`).
- [ ] **Real UK Mechanics**: Explains what happens in practice (notice/PENP tax split, accrued holiday, agreed references, solicitor privilege), not abstract textbook theory.
- [ ] **Primary Citations**: Links or cites exact UK statutes (`legislation.gov.uk` - ERA 1996, ITEPA 2003, SI 2026/310) and ACAS Code 4.
- [ ] **Zero Fabricated Metrics**: No invented user counts, star ratings, or "average" marketing claims without verified data.
- [ ] **Zero AI Slop**: No padded openings, no repetitive listicle transitions, and no summary sentences that repeat the previous line.

## 3. Prescriptive vs Descriptive Matrix

| Funnel Context | ❌ Prescriptive (Unauthorised / Risky) | ✅ Descriptive (Objective / Compliant) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Negotiation Section** | "What to say to your employer" | "Questions to consider with a solicitor" |
| **Target Figure** | "You should ask for £19,673" | "Calculated illustrative negotiation range: £19,673" |
| **Zero Offer State** | "The current offer is £0" / "Offer: £0" | "No employer offer recorded" (suppress counter-offer) |
| **Offer Evaluation** | "Do not accept this offer" / "Is this offer fair?" | "Offer is below the calculated statutory baseline" / "How does the offer compare?" |
| **Statutory Entitlement** | "Statutory floor" / "Guaranteed payout" | "Calculated statutory baseline" |
| **ACAS Consideration** | "ACAS 10-day clock" / "10-day deadline" | "ACAS Code 4 guidance (10 calendar days)" |
| **Legal Contribution** | "Your employer is required to pay £500" | "Employers standardly contribute £350 to £500+ plus VAT upon completion" |
| **Email Template** | "Without Prejudice: Proposal" | "Discussion Agenda / Note of Figures" |
