# Primary Legal Authorities & Statutory References

## 1. Employment Rights Act 1996 (ERA 1996)

### Section 203(3) — Validity of Compromise / Settlement Agreements
Under Section 203(1), any provision in an agreement is void in so far as it purports to exclude or limit the operation of any provision of the Act.
Section 203(3) provides the strict statutory exception for settlement agreements. All 6 conditions must be satisfied:
1. **In Writing**: The agreement must be in writing.
2. **Particular Complaints**: The agreement must relate to the particular proceedings or particular complaint. Blanket waivers of unknown future claims are unenforceable (*BCCI v Ali* [2001] UKHL 8; *Bathgate v Technip Singapore PTE Ltd* [2023] CSIH 48).
3. **Independent Advice**: The employee must have received advice from a relevant independent adviser as to the terms and effect of the proposed agreement and, in particular, its effect on their ability to pursue their rights before an employment tribunal.
   - Relevant advisers under s.203(4): Qualified lawyer/solicitor holding a current practising certificate; officer, official or member of an independent trade union; advice centre worker.
4. **Professional Indemnity Insurance**: There must be in force a contract of insurance, or an indemnity provided for members of a profession, covering the risk of a claim by the employee in respect of loss arising in consequence of the advice.
5. **Adviser Identification**: The agreement must identify the adviser.
6. **Statutory Statement**: The agreement must state that the conditions regulating settlement agreements under the relevant statutory provisions are satisfied.

### Section 111A — Pre-Termination Negotiations vs Without Prejudice
- **Section 111A(1)**: Evidence of pre-termination negotiations is inadmissible in proceedings on a complaint under section 111 (unfair dismissal).
- **Critical Exclusions under s.111A(4) & s.111A(5)**:
  - Protection applies strictly to standard unfair dismissal claims.
  - It does NOT apply to claims of discrimination, harassment, victimisation (Equality Act 2010), whistleblowing (s.103A ERA 1996), trade union membership, or breach of contract.
  - Inadmissibility is lost if there has been "improper behaviour" by either party (e.g., undue pressure, harassment, false threats of dismissal before any capability process has begun).
- **Common Law Without Prejudice**: Requires an existing dispute between the parties (*Framlington Group Ltd v Staveley*). Without prejudice negotiations protect all claims, but fail if no dispute existed at the time of communication.

### Section 162 & 227 — Statutory Redundancy Pay Calculation
- **Weekly Pay Cap**: England, Scotland & Wales: **£751/week** (effective 6 April 2026 under SI 2026/310). Northern Ireland: **£783/week** (effective 6 April 2026 under SR 2026).
- **Multipliers**:
  - 0.5 week's pay for each full year of service under age 22.
  - 1.0 week's pay for each full year of service between ages 22 and 40 (inclusive).
  - 1.5 week's pay for each full year of service aged 41 and older.
- **Maximum Service**: Strictly capped at **20 years** (ERA 1996 s.162(3)).
- **Maximum Statutory Redundancy Pay (GB)**: 20 × 1.5 × £751 = **£22,530**.
- **Maximum Statutory Redundancy Pay (NI)**: 20 × 1.5 × £783 = **£23,490**.

### Section 124 — Compensatory Award Limit for Unfair Dismissal
- Effective 6 April 2026 (SI 2026/310): Maximum compensatory award is **£123,543** (or 52 weeks' actual gross salary, whichever is lower, under s.124(1ZA)).

## 2. Equality Act 2010 & Vento Bands (2026/27)

### Section 124 — Uncapped Compensation
Tribunal compensation for discrimination, harassment, and victimisation under the Equality Act 2010 is **uncapped**.

### Vento Guidelines (Ninth Addendum, applicable from 6 April 2026)
Presidential Guidance issued by the Presidents of the Employment Tribunals:
- **Lower Band (£1,300 to £12,600)**: For isolated or one-off incidents of discrimination or harassment.
- **Middle Band (£12,600 to £37,700)**: For serious cases that do not merit an award in the upper band.
- **Upper Band (£37,700 to £62,900)**: For the most serious cases, such as where there has been a prolonged campaign of discriminatory harassment.
- **Exceptional Cases**: Awards can exceed **£62,900** in the most egregious cases.
- **Governing Rule**: The applicable Vento band is determined strictly by the **date the ET1 claim form is presented to the Employment Tribunal**.

## 3. ACAS Code of Practice 4 on Settlement Agreements

- **Time to Consider**: Paragraph 12 of the ACAS Code of Practice states that employers should allow a reasonable period of time for the employee to consider the proposed terms and obtain advice. As a general rule, a minimum period of **10 calendar days** should be allowed unless agreed otherwise.
- **Improper Behaviour**: Giving less than a reasonable period, or threatening dismissal before any formal process has begun, constitutes improper behaviour, rendering pre-termination negotiations admissible in tribunal.

## 4. Legal Services Act 2007 (ss.12–14) & SRA Regulatory Boundaries

- **Reserved Legal Activities**: Section 12 identifies reserved legal activities (the exercise of a right of audience, conduct of litigation, reserved instrument activities, probate, notarial, administration of oaths).
- **Statutory Boundary for SettlementCheck**: SettlementCheck operates strictly as a decision-support and statutory calculation tool. It does NOT provide legal advice under Section 203(3) ERA 1996. All output must remind the employee that statutory legal advice must be provided by their instructed SRA-regulated solicitor.
