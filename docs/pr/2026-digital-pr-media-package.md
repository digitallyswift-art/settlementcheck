## Executive Press Briefing

The **Annual UK Employment Settlement Index 2026** provides the first empirical benchmark of workplace severance packages following the implementation of the Employment Rights (Increase of Limits) Order 2026 (SI 2026/310), which raised the statutory redundancy pay cap to £751 per week from 6 April 2026.

Drawing on aggregated k-anonymity platform telemetry (k &ge; 50) synthesized with official datasets from the Office for National Statistics (ONS ASHE) and Ministry of Justice (MoJ) Employment Tribunal statistics, the report uncovers significant shifts in commercial settlement behaviour:

1. **The 38-Year Severance Tax Freeze:** The £30,000 tax-free exemption threshold under Section 403 of ITEPA 2003 has remained unindexed since 1988. In real terms (RPI), it has lost more than 67% of its purchasing power, subjecting middle earners to aggressive fiscal drag.
2. **The 540-Day Tribunal Queue:** In London Central, the median wait from ET1 lodgement to a final merits hearing has reached 540 days (approaching 18 months). With employer defense costs escalating to £18,000 - £45,000+ for a standard multi-day hearing, commercial employers are settling claims at substantial multiples over statutory floors to mitigate litigation exposure.
3. **The Sector Severance Divide:** Exit payouts in Financial Services and Banking average **3.4x statutory redundancy pay**, while Retail and Consumer Goods average just **1.4x**.
4. **PENP Compliance Failure:** Nearly one in three (31%) initial draft settlement agreements miscalculate Post-Employment Notice Pay (PENP) under Section 402D ITEPA 2003, leaving employers exposed to the reformed 15.0% Class 1A employer NIC rate and former employees vulnerable to retrospective HMRC tax recovery.
