# SRA Rule 5.1 Panel Compliance Addendum

**Document Reference:** `docs/compliance/sra-rule-5-1-panel-addendum.md`

**Applicability:** Mandatory binding contractual addendum between Digitally Swift Ltd (t/a SettlementCheck.co.uk) and all participating SRA-regulated Law Firms ("Panel Firms")  
**Compliance Review Cycle:** Annual / Mandatory re-certification upon practicing certificate renewal

## 1. Regulatory Context & Core Purpose

This Addendum establishes the binding regulatory conditions governing the introduction of prospective clients by Digitally Swift Ltd ("SettlementCheck", "the Platform") to independent solicitors and law firms regulated by the Solicitors Regulation Authority ("the Panel Firm").

Both SettlementCheck and the Panel Firm acknowledge that:
1. Under **Rule 5.1 of the SRA Code of Conduct for Solicitors, RELs and RFLs** and **Rule 5.1 of the SRA Code of Conduct for Firms**, solicitors must ensure that any referral, introduction, or fee-sharing arrangement does not jeopardize their independence, integrity, or duty to act in the best interests of their clients.
2. Settlement agreements under **Section 203(3) of the Employment Rights Act 1996 (ERA 1996)** require independent legal advice from a qualified adviser covered by professional indemnity insurance. If the independence of the adviser is compromised by undisclosed or improper commercial steering, the statutory waiver of claims may be challenged or rendered void, causing catastrophic harm to the employee and legal exposure to both parties.
3. SettlementCheck is an independent digital decision-support platform operated by Digitally Swift Ltd, a commercial technology enterprise incorporated in England and Wales. SettlementCheck is not a law firm, is not authorized by the SRA, and does not provide reserved legal activities under the Legal Services Act 2007.

To ensure unyielding adherence to SRA Standards and Regulations, this Addendum sets forth non-negotiable principles of absolute independence, transparent commercial administration, and client protection.

## 2. Absolute Professional Independence & Unfettered Duty of Care

### 2.1 Sole Duty of Care to the Employee Client
From the moment of client introduction, the Panel Firm and its instructed fee earners owe an absolute, undivided, and unfettered duty of care solely and exclusively to the introduced employee client.

### 2.2 Complete Absence of Platform Influence
SettlementCheck shall have no standing, right, authority, or influence over:
- The legal advice, merits assessment, or opinions given to the employee client.
- The negotiation strategy, valuation assessments, or tactical counter-proposals formulated by the Panel Firm.
- The recommendation whether to accept, reject, renegotiate, or litigate an employer settlement proposal.
- The drafting or modification of agreement terms, waiver schedules, or settlement clauses.

### 2.3 Freedom to Advise Rejection or Litigation
The Panel Firm is explicitly instructed and contractually obligated to provide robust, objective, and unbiased legal advice. Where an employer offer is unfair, below statutory minimum entitlements, fails to compensate for valid statutory discrimination or detriment claims, or where tribunal litigation represents the client's optimal path, the Panel Firm must advise the client accordingly without regard to the commercial interests of SettlementCheck or the employer.

### 2.4 Prohibition of Interference
SettlementCheck covenants that it will never seek to review draft legal advice, attend confidential consultations, request privileged case files, or penalize any Panel Firm for advising an employee client to reject a settlement offer or terminate negotiations.

### 3.1 Strict Prohibition of Prohibited Referral Fees
Pursuant to SRA Rule 5.1(a) and 5.1(b):
1. **No Success-Based Referral Commissions**: SettlementCheck does not charge, demand, or accept any percentage, commission, success fee, or contingent slice of any financial settlement achieved by the Panel Firm or client.
2. **No Claim Outcome Contingency**: The commercial fee paid by the Panel Firm to SettlementCheck is strictly non-contingent upon whether:
   - The employee client ultimately instructs the Panel Firm.
   - The employer pays a settlement sum or legal fee contribution.
   - The settlement agreement is signed, amended, aborted, or litigated in the Employment Tribunal.
3. **No Improper Steering**: SettlementCheck operates automated, geographic, and capacity-based routing. The platform does not steer clients to specific firms based on commercial kickbacks, premium bidding, or reciprocal referral promises.

### 3.2 SRA Rule 5.1(c) Mandatory Written Client Disclosure
Under SRA Rule 5.1(c), solicitors must inform clients in writing of any financial or other interest that an introducer has in referring the client. 

The Panel Firm covenants that its formal Client Care Letter (CCL) sent to every client introduced by SettlementCheck shall include the standard disclosure specified in Section 4 of this Addendum.

## 5. Employee Freedom of Choice & Non-Exclusivity

### 5.1 Unrestricted Employee Choice
The platform prominently informs all users that:
1. They are under no legal or commercial obligation to instruct the introduced Panel Firm.
2. They retain the absolute statutory right under Section 203(3) ERA 1996 to instruct any qualified independent adviser, law firm, or certified trade union representative.
3. They may terminate their engagement with the Panel Firm at any stage in accordance with the Panel Firm's terms of business.

### 5.2 Anti-Lock-In Covenant
The Panel Firm covenants that it will never represent to the client that using SettlementCheck's panel is mandatory, condition the validity of statutory formulas on using the panel, or penalize a client who seeks a second opinion or switches representation.

## 6. Panel Firm Compliance Warranties & Regulatory Reporting

The Panel Firm warrants, represents, and undertakes to SettlementCheck as an ongoing contractual condition that:
1. **SRA Authorisation**: It is and remains fully authorized and regulated by the Solicitors Regulation Authority as a recognized body, licensed body (ABS), or recognized sole practice.
2. **Practising Certificates**: Every fee earner advising on introduced settlement matters holds a current, valid, unrestricted Practising Certificate issued by the SRA.
3. **Professional Indemnity Insurance (PII)**: It maintains qualifying Professional Indemnity Insurance complying with the SRA Minimum Terms and Conditions (MTC) with a minimum indemnity limit of not less than £3,000,000 (or £2,000,000 for sole practices) covering employment law advice and statutory Section 203(3) certifications.
4. **Immediate Incident Notification**: The Panel Firm shall notify SettlementCheck in writing within two (2) business days in the event that:
   - Any SRA investigation, disciplinary proceedings, or regulatory intervention is initiated against the firm or a participating employment fee earner.
   - Its PII policy is cancelled, non-renewed, or subject to a material coverage restriction.
   - Any complaint relating to introducer transparency, SRA Rule 5.1, or conflict of interest is lodged by an introduced client with the Legal Ombudsman (LeO) or SRA.

---

## 7. Suspension & Termination for Regulatory Breach

SettlementCheck maintains an automatic, zero-tolerance suspension protocol:
1. **Immediate Suspension**: If SettlementCheck receives evidence of SRA sanction, PII lapse, or failure to deliver required SRA Rule 5.1 disclosures in client care documentation, lead routing to the affected Panel Firm will be suspended within 15 minutes.
2. **Termination**: Material breach of this Addendum shall result in immediate termination of the panel partnership, removal from the platform directory, and reporting to the Solicitors Regulation Authority where required by the SRA Standards and Regulations.
